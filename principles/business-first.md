# Business First

Define Business Unit, user, decision, existing workflow, bottleneck, error consequences and success KPI before choosing a model.

A public dataset is a proxy for a business scenario. State what it represents and what it cannot validate. Use **Observed**, **Assumed**, **To Validate** for material claims.

Success includes processing time, review workload, failure recovery and cost, not just model scores. Start with a rule or traditional ML baseline; add AI only with a measured benefit.
