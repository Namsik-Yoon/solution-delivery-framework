# Minimum Viable Delivery

Deliver one user decision through one executable workflow. A deterministic demo, meaningful tests, reproducible environment, failure behavior and runbook are the first gate.

Defer extra models, notebooks, databases and agents until a requirement justifies them. Public-proxy evaluation is distinct from production validation. Never claim production readiness from a synthetic demo.
